### The Long Trail Ahead

Silver City gave WeatherMule a reason to exist.

The weather station keeps watching the mountain whether the internet is working or not. Every few minutes it produces another observation: temperature, wind, rain, humidity, and the other measurements that describe what is happening outside.

Those observations have a destination.

The Pack Station is where mules deliver their cargo. It maintains the long-term weather record and exposes it to the public. But between a weather station on a mountain and a pack station somewhere down the trail are all the things that can go wrong: lost WiFi, unavailable internet, power interruptions, failed connections, and equipment that simply refuses to cooperate.

WeatherMule lives in the middle.

Its job is to accept the weather station's observations, place them safely into local Packboxes, and attempt to carry them to the Pack Station.

Sometimes the trail is open and the trip is easy.

Sometimes it isn't.

When observations do not arrive, the Pack Station eventually sees the gap in its weather record. We call that gap a **hole**. The Pack Station can then send WeatherMule back through its Packboxes looking for the missing observations.

That gives us the basic cast of characters:

- **The Weather Station** observes the weather.
- **A Weather Observation** is the cargo.
- **WeatherMule** carries the cargo.
- **A Packbox** keeps the cargo safe until it is needed.
- **Pack Station** keeps the long-term record and knows what has arrived.
- **A Hole** is a gap in that record that may send the mule looking for missing cargo.

The actual journey has a few more twists and turns, but at its heart WeatherMule is that simple.

The weather station creates the cargo.  
The mule protects it.  
The pack station keeps the books.

Now we can follow an observation down the trail.
